Privacy

Privacy Policy

Draft for the intended Mac App Store release · Last updated 12 October 2026. Recheck against the final signed build before publication.

What the app uses

Quota Otter requests account login state and rate-limit data from the Codex helper included in the app through Codex App Server. The Codex CLI may contact OpenAI under the account you signed into separately. Quota Otter does not read Codex authentication files, your code, prompts, conversation content, or an API key.

Stored on your Mac

App preferences include reminder switches, low-quota threshold, pet position, and selected character. Imported character images are copied into local Application Support. Current quota results are held in app memory while running. Local notification identifiers and any seen TIBO post IDs are saved to prevent duplicate reminders. The app has no developer-operated account service, analytics, or advertising SDK.

Network and third parties

When community notices are enabled, the app requests the public TIBO status endpoint at tibo.cc about every three minutes while running. The request does not intentionally include your Codex account ID or quota data. As with any web request, the service may receive network metadata such as your IP address. Opening an original post takes you to that external site. Turn the community switch off to stop TIBO requests.

Notifications and your choices

macOS delivers quota and community alerts locally. You can disable each alert in the app, or revoke notification permission in System Settings. You can restore the built-in otter to remove the copied custom character. Deleting the app may leave its sandbox container and macOS preferences until you remove them; contact support for help.

Contact

For privacy questions, open a support request.

简体中文

隐私政策

拟用于 Mac App Store 版本；更新于 2026 年 10 月 12 日。发布前需根据最终签名构建核对。

应用使用的数据

额度水獭通过应用内置的 Codex 辅助程序的 App Server 查询登录状态与额度。Codex CLI 可能按你单独登录的账号与 OpenAI 通信。本应用不读取 Codex 认证文件、代码、提示词、对话内容或 API Key。

本机保存

提醒开关、低额度阈值、宠物位置、所选角色等设置保存在本机。导入的角色图片复制到本机 Application Support；额度结果在应用运行时保存在内存中。通知标识与已见过的 TIBO 原帖 ID 用于避免重复提醒。开发者没有自建账号服务、分析埋点或广告 SDK。

网络与第三方

启用社区预告时，运行中的应用约每 3 分钟请求一次 TIBO 公开状态接口。请求不会主动附带你的 Codex 账号 ID 或个人额度；网络服务仍可能看到 IP 等连接信息。点击原帖会打开外部网站。关闭社区预告开关即可停止 TIBO 查询。

通知与选择

额度与社区提醒由 macOS 在本机发送。你可在应用内逐项关闭提醒，或在系统设置中撤销通知权限。恢复内置水獭会删除复制的自定义角色。删除应用后，沙盒容器及系统偏好设置可能仍保留；需要帮助可联系支持。

隐私问题请提交支持请求。
