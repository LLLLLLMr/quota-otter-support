Privacy

Privacy Policy

Draft for the intended Mac App Store release · Last updated 15 October 2026. Recheck against the final signed build before publication.

What the app uses

Quota Otter requests account login state and rate-limit data from the Codex helper included in the app through Codex App Server. The included Codex helper contacts OpenAI after you sign in through the app. Quota Otter's UI does not read your code, prompts, conversation content, or an API key. The bundled Codex helper manages its login inside the app sandbox.

Stored on your Mac

App preferences include reminder switches, low-quota threshold, pet position, and selected character. Imported character images are copied into local Application Support. Current quota results are held in app memory while running. Local notification identifiers are saved to prevent duplicate reminders. The app has no developer-operated account service, analytics, or advertising SDK.

Network and third parties

The included Codex helper contacts OpenAI for sign-in and quota checks. The Mac App Store build does not request community reset feeds. As with any web request, OpenAI may receive network metadata such as your IP address. Quota Otter's developer does not operate a server that receives your account credentials or quota results.

Notifications and your choices

macOS delivers quota alerts locally. You can disable each alert in the app, or revoke notification permission in System Settings. You can restore the built-in otter to remove the copied custom character. Deleting the app may leave its sandbox container and macOS preferences until you remove them; contact support for help.

Contact

For privacy questions, open a support request.

简体中文

隐私政策

拟用于 Mac App Store 版本；更新于 2026 年 10 月 15 日。发布前需根据最终签名构建核对。

应用使用的数据

额度水獭通过应用内置的 Codex 辅助程序的 App Server 查询登录状态与额度。内置 Codex 辅助程序会在你通过应用登录后与 OpenAI 通信。本应用界面不读取代码、提示词、对话内容或 API Key；内置 Codex 辅助程序在应用沙盒内管理登录。

本机保存

提醒开关、低额度阈值、宠物位置、所选角色等设置保存在本机。导入的角色图片复制到本机 Application Support；额度结果在应用运行时保存在内存中。通知标识用于避免重复提醒。开发者没有自建账号服务、分析埋点或广告 SDK。

网络与第三方

内置 Codex 辅助程序会与 OpenAI 通信，完成登录和额度查询。Mac App Store 版本不请求社区重置消息源。与其他网络请求一样，OpenAI 可能收到 IP 地址等连接信息。额度水獭的开发者没有接收账号凭据或额度结果的服务器。

通知与选择

额度提醒由 macOS 在本机发送。你可在应用内逐项关闭提醒，或在系统设置中撤销通知权限。恢复内置水獭会删除复制的自定义角色。删除应用后，沙盒容器及系统偏好设置可能仍保留；需要帮助可联系支持。

联系

隐私问题请提交支持请求。
